DrivePod – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

DrivePod is an audio player for audiobooks and podcasts stored in your Google Drive or on your phone.

What DrivePod accesses

  • Only the folders you pick. You choose each folder with Android’s own folder picker — from your phone’s storage or from a cloud app such as Google Drive. DrivePod can read the files in those folders (and their sub-folders) to list and play your audio. It cannot see anything else, and it never modifies, deletes, or uploads files.
  • No Google account access. DrivePod does not ask you to sign in and does not request access to your Google account or Google Drive. When you pick a Google Drive folder, the Google Drive app on your phone provides the files under Google’s own terms.

What DrivePod stores

All data stays on your device:

  • the list of folders you added, listening positions, playback speed, and app settings
  • audio files you choose to download for offline listening

What DrivePod does NOT do

  • It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • It has no servers and does not use the internet itself (the app does not hold Android’s internet permission); nothing is sent to the developer.
  • It contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Removing your data

  • Remove a folder from DrivePod (Library → ⋮ → Remove from library) to revoke its access.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data and downloads.

Contact

Questions: use the contact email shown on DrivePod’s Google Play page.
