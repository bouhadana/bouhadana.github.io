DrivePod – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

DrivePod is an audio player for audiobooks and podcasts stored in your Google Drive or on your phone.

What DrivePod accesses

  • Google Drive (read-only). When you connect your Google account, DrivePod asks for read-only access (drive.readonly). It is used only to list your folders and audio files and to stream or download those files to your device. DrivePod never modifies, deletes, or uploads anything in your Drive.
  • Your Google account name, email and photo. Shown in the app's Settings screen so you know which account is connected.
  • Folders on your phone that you choose. Only folders you explicitly select are read, to play the audio files in them.

What DrivePod stores

All data stays on your device:

  • listening positions, playback speed, and app settings
  • audio files you choose to download for offline listening

What DrivePod does NOT do

  • It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • It has no servers of its own; your data is never sent to the developer.
  • It contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Communication with Google Drive happens directly between your device and Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, under Google's Privacy Policy.

DrivePod's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect your account in Settings → Sign out, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data and downloads.

Contact

Questions: use the contact email shown on DrivePod's Google Play page.
