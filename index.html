DrivePod

Audiobooks and podcasts from Google Drive, on Android.

DrivePod plays the MP3 and audio files you keep in Google Drive (your own folders, folders shared with you, and shared drives) or on your phone.

  • Streams from Drive file by file, or downloads for offline listening
  • Remembers where you stopped in every file
  • Playback speed from 0.5× to 3× in 0.1 steps, with quick presets
  • No sign-in: add folders with Android’s folder picker; DrivePod only reads the folders you pick
